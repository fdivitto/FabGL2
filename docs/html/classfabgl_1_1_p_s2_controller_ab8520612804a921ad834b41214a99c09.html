Initializes the PS/2 device controller.

Initializes the PS/2 controller, assigning GPIOs to the DATA and CLK lines.

Since the PS/2 ports are handled by the ULP coprocessor, only a few GPIOs can be used: GPIO_NUM_2, GPIO_NUM_4, GPIO_NUM_12 (with some limitations), GPIO_NUM_13, GPIO_NUM_14, GPIO_NUM_15, GPIO_NUM_25, GPIO_NUM_26, GPIO_NUM_27, GPIO_NUM_32 and GPIO_NUM_33.

Parameters
port0_clkGPIOGPIO of the clock line of PS/2 port 0.
port0_datGPIOGPIO of the data line of PS/2 port 0.
port1_clkGPIOGPIO of the clock line of PS/2 port 1 (GPIO_UNUSED to disable).
port1_datGPIOGPIO of the data line of PS/2 port 1 (GPIO_UNUSED to disable).
